Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions.

Need help choosing a starting point? Ask AKH is available from the lower corner of the page.

How do we decide which service is right for us?

Start with the challenge or opportunity you want to address. AKH can discuss your goals, existing systems and constraints, then help identify a sensible next step.

Can you work with systems we already use?

Yes. Existing workflows and platforms can be part of the discovery and scoping conversation. A new build is not assumed to be the right answer.

Can I combine more than one service?

Yes. Software, AI, search, design and specialist staffing can be considered together when that fits the work.

How is a project scoped and priced?

Costs depend on the goals, scope, integrations and delivery needs. The first step is to understand the work before discussing a tailored estimate.

Can AKH provide technical specialists?

Tech staffing can support needs across applied AI, frontend and backend development, cloud and platform engineering, and broader IT roles shaped around the project.
